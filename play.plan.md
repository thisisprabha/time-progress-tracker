## Full Description (≤4000 chars)
Stay motivated by seeing how much time is **truly** left — not just how much is done. **Time Progress Tracker** shows live progress for your day, week, month, year, and business quarters (Q1–Q4), plus custom event countdowns.

**What you get:**
- **Dynamic Home Screen Widgets:** Choose between a sleek light or dark theme for your large, customizable widget. See your progress at a glance without opening the app.
- **Personalized Perspective:** Select a "half-full" (time done) or "half-empty" (time left) mindset to match your motivation style.
- **Flexible Tracking Modes:** Track your day in 24-hour mode or focus on your 9-to-5 office hours.
- **Customizable Display:** Choose up to three items to display on your main screen and widget, including Today, Week, Month, Quarter, Year, and your own Custom Events.
- **Custom Event Countdown:** Add important dates like birthdays, anniversaries, or project deadlines and track the days remaining or elapsed.
- **Weekly Notifications:** Get a summary of your selected progress items delivered directly to your notifications every Monday at 9 AM.
- **Simple & Intuitive Design:** A clean, minimalist interface keeps you focused on what matters most – your time.

**Why Time Progress Tracker?**
Your time is a precious, limited resource. This app helps you visualize that reality, encouraging you to make the most of every moment. Whether you're tracking personal goals, work deadlines, or simply want to be more mindful of your day, **Time Progress Tracker** empowers you to stay motivated and achieve more.

**Download now and start making every second count!**
